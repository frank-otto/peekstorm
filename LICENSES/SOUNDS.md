## Music – VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0 1.0)
https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE – trimmed, faded and loudness-matched; the game re-pitches the nearest recorded note. Original music written for this game (a tiptoeing hide-and-seek theme at 96–114 BPM and a menu nocturne), sequenced note by note.
